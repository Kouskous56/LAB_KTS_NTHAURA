# Cau 2: Xac dinh X, Y, Z, W trong 4 mach

Nguyen tac: switch **ho (OFF)** -> nut duoc keo len +5V -> tin hieu = **1**.
Switch **dong (ON)** -> nut noi GND -> tin hieu = **0**.

| Mach | Switch 1 | Switch 2 | Switch 3 | Switch 4 | X, Y, Z, W |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ON (den) | ON (den) | ON (den) | ON (den) | X=Y=Z=W=0 |
| 2 | OFF (trang) | OFF (trang) | ON (den) | ON (den) | X=1, Y=1, Z=0, W=0 |
| 3 | OFF (trang) | ON (den) | OFF (trang) | ON (den) | X=1, Y=0, Z=1, W=0 |
| 4 | OFF (trang) | OFF (trang) | OFF (trang) | OFF (trang) | X=Y=Z=W=1 |

*(Doi chieu truc tiep voi hinh ve goc de xac nhan vi tri o den/trang cua tung mach.)*
