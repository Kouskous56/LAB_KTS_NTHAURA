# Cau 3: Trang thai LED khi f1, f2, f3, f4 = 0, 1, 1, 0

**Mach trai (active-low):** LED sang khi f = 0.
**Mach phai (active-high):** LED sang khi f = 1.

| Tin hieu | Gia tri | Mach | LED |
|---|---|---|---|
| f1 | 0 | trai (active-low) | **sang** |
| f2 | 1 | trai (active-low) | **tat** |
| f3 | 1 | phai (active-high) | **sang** |
| f4 | 0 | phai (active-high) | **tat** |
